# Nigeria Treasury & Digital Asset Signal-to-Revenue Pack v0.1

## Canonical decision

Bitcoin/crypto is not a standalone AfrIAgenesis product and monitoring is not the deliverable. The Nigeria market is the primary commercial wedge for converting global-liquidity and digital-asset signals into measurable treasury savings, compliance readiness and recurring software revenue.

## Product ladder

1. **Nigeria Treasury & Digital Asset Exposure Diagnostic™**
   - Target: importers/distributors, industrial groups, fintechs/PSPs, then banks.
   - Outcome: quantify NGN/USD exposure, FX leakage, cross-border payment cost, settlement delays, stablecoin/crypto exposure, regulatory/compliance gaps and actionable savings.
   - Price anchors: EUR 5k-15k depending on scope.

2. **Treasury & Global Liquidity Intelligence Cockpit™**
   - Pilot: EUR 15k-30k.
   - Institutional annual licence: EUR 25k-75k.
   - Enterprise/API: custom.

## Mandatory revenue chain

Signal -> Qualified Nigerian account -> Pain quantified -> Diagnostic offer -> Proposal -> Payment -> Delivery -> Savings proof -> Cockpit upsell -> Annual licence -> R.E.M.E -> Scale/Correct/Kill.

A market signal without a named ICP, commercial next action and path to cash is non-productive.

## Nigeria problem model

The diagnostic must score at minimum:
- NGN/USD exposure and volatility sensitivity.
- FX acquisition cost and hidden spread/leakage.
- International transfer fees and correspondent-bank friction.
- Supplier settlement time and working-capital impact.
- Stablecoin usage/exposure and counterparty/custody risk.
- AML/CFT/KYC controls and audit evidence.
- SEC/CBN regulatory perimeter and escalation requirements.
- PAPSS/AfCFTA relevance for eligible African trade flows.
- Payment-rail alternatives and decision rationale.
- Estimated annual savings, risk reduction and payback.

## Product architecture

Inputs -> Treasury Exposure Engine -> Payment Rail Comparator -> Regulatory Rules Engine -> Risk & Controls Engine -> Savings/ROI Engine -> Recommendation Layer -> Human Approval Gate -> Evidence Ledger -> R.E.M.E.

### Data boundaries
- Read-only market, treasury and transaction inputs by default.
- No custody of client funds.
- No autonomous trading.
- No execution of regulated financial activity without explicit legal/regulatory qualification and approved partner rail.
- Recommendations must preserve provenance, confidence and human approval.

## Commercial ICP priority

### ICP-1 Importers / distributors
Trigger: recurring USD supplier payments, material FX leakage, delayed settlement, high working-capital cost.
Buyer: CFO, Finance Director, Treasurer, CEO/Owner.
Primary promise: identify and quantify avoidable treasury/payment losses.

### ICP-2 Fintech / PSP
Trigger: cross-border volume growth, stablecoin exposure, compliance burden, multi-rail routing complexity.
Buyer: CEO, COO, Head of Compliance, Head of Treasury/Payments.
Primary promise: improve routing economics and control evidence without adding ungoverned crypto risk.

### ICP-3 Banks / financial institutions
Trigger: treasury optimisation, institutional digital-asset policy, cross-border infrastructure and compliance.
Buyer: Treasurer, CIO, CRO, Head of Digital, Head of Compliance.
Primary promise: controlled institutional intelligence and decision support.

## Revenue Economics Gate

The offer cannot be marked `sellable` until these fields are populated:
- Named ICP segment.
- Pain hypothesis with financial metric.
- Offer price.
- Delivery cost estimate.
- Gross-margin target.
- Sales channel.
- Sales script/CTA.
- CRM owner.
- Payment method.
- First 20 target accounts.
- Proof plan.

Initial economic targets:
- Diagnostic gross margin >= 65%.
- Pilot gross margin >= 60%.
- Annual licence gross margin >= 75%.
- CAC payback target <= 3 months for diagnostic-led acquisition.
- Diagnostic-to-pilot conversion target >= 25%.
- Pilot-to-annual conversion target >= 40%.

1. 20 qualified Nigerian accounts identified.
2. 10 outbound conversations launched.

## Kill criteria

Correct or kill the offer if, after a controlled outreach sample, there is no evidence of willingness to pay, no quantifiable treasury pain, or delivery economics fail the Revenue Economics Gate.
