## Canonical invariant

No AfrIAgenesis® growth is considered healthy when it increases faster than the system's capacity to document, secure, execute, finance, support and reproduce that growth.

Growth is not an autonomous objective. The objective is the capacity to absorb, control, document and reproduce growth.

## Mandatory operating rules

1. **Build-before-need™** — critical deployment, backup, rollback, monitoring, security, continuity, support, access and recovery capabilities must exist before they become urgent.
2. **Document-while-green™** — runbooks, ADRs, procedures, dependencies, access maps, evidence, RACI, recovery plans and key decisions are documented while the system is healthy.
3. **Hire-for-gaps™** — humans and AI agents are added to measurable capability gaps, not to duplicate strengths already present.
4. **Scale-after-structure™** — no product, country, channel or acquisition budget is accelerated until operational, economic, technical and governance structure is ready.

## Scale Readiness Gate™

The Scale Readiness Gate™ is a transverse GENOME™ and Genesis Release-to-Revenue Control Plane™ gate. It runs after commercial proof and operational measurement, immediately before any material SCALE decision.

Minimum controls:

- documentation and runbooks;
- operational automation;
- security, access control and auditability;
- observability, alerting and SLO/SLA readiness;
- rollback, backup, disaster recovery and business continuity;
- key-person and critical-provider dependency;
- support and incident-handling capacity;
- infrastructure capacity and headroom;
- quality and error rate;
- gross margin, unit economics, cash-flow and cost of scale;
- revenue/customer concentration;
- legal and regulatory compliance;
- repeatability across clients, countries or instances without manual reconstruction.

## Gate decisions

- **GO SCALE** only when there is no unresolved critical blocker and structural capacity is evidenced.
- **HOLD / CORRECT** when revenue exists but the system cannot absorb acceleration without disproportionate risk.
- Revenue growth alone never proves Scale Readiness.
- Any unresolved failure involving security, rollback, continuity, compliance, unit economics/margin or critical dependency blocks SCALE until corrected.

## Anti-duplication rule

This doctrine does **not** create a new product silo. Scale Readiness Gate™ complements Economic Viability Gate™, Revenue Proof Gate™, M6, S7+, M8 and external review.

- Economic Viability asks: is the business economically viable?
- Revenue Proof asks: will the market pay?
- Scale Readiness asks: can the organization absorb growth without becoming fragile?

## Execution infrastructure

AfrIAgenesis Deploy™, AfrIAgenesis Domain Manager™ and AfrIAgenesis Release Center™ are the execution mechanisms that provide repeatable build/deployment, domain and HTTPS management, availability checks, release traceability and delivery evidence required by this doctrine.

## Required evidence

Every material SCALE decision must generate a **Scale Readiness Evidence Pack** containing at least:

- readiness score and gaps;
- critical blockers;
- M6 / S7+ / M8 evidence;
- rollback / backup / DR status;
- unit economics and cash-flow implications;
- support capacity;
- critical dependencies;
- GO / HOLD / CORRECT decision;
- owner and due date for residual actions.

## Runtime implementation contract

A future runtime implementation must expose a governed `scale_readiness_gate` capability with auditable inputs, evidence references, blocker severity, decision output and R.E.M.E™ feedback. No runtime component may declare a product `SCALE_READY` solely from revenue growth.
